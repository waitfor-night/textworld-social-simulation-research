## 一句话要点

WVS-grounded agents often fail to express assigned values before any long-run drift occurs.

## 研究对象

- **世界或环境：** WVS-grounded longitudinal value discussions
- **智能体：** 1,200 personas across about 4,000 conversations and three models
- **观察与动作：** value expression, drift, dialogue style, and topic responses
- **目标与交互协议：** repeated value-laden discussion with demographic and communication-style ablations

## 论文做了什么

Tests value faithfulness, drift, and conversational realism in a social simulation intended to proxy human participants.

## 证据与发现

The canonical abstract reports more than half of personas failing assigned profiles initially and 2–7% drifting after repeated conversations; simulated distributions still deviate from WVS profiles.

## 可复用模式

Measure initialization fidelity separately from longitudinal drift and stylistic realism.

## 局限性与开放问题

The synthetic personas, topics, and three-model comparison do not establish validity for real populations.

## 与本索引的关系

Direct social-simulation validity baseline for the persona and deliberation cluster.
