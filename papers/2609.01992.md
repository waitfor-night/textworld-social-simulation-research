## One-line takeaway

ClaimReceipt makes an evaluation claim carry a verifiable, claim-relative receipt so missing or insufficient evidence is reported as inconclusive instead of silently passing.

## Research object

- **World or environment:** Buyer-seller transaction records and a prospective assignment epoch used to test evidence-backed agent evaluations.
- **Agents:** Task agents emit signed manifests and receipts; an auditor verifies claims against the recorded evidence and coverage rules.
- **Observations and actions:** The verifier checks referenced records, semantic fault conditions, and whether the claimed population is fully covered; it returns `PASS`, `INVALID`, or `INCONCLUSIVE`.
- **Goals and interaction protocol:** Prevent unsupported aggregate claims and distinguish an invalid result from a result that cannot be audited because evidence is missing.

## What the paper does

The paper specifies claim-relative receipts and a verifier, then evaluates historical records (CR-2) and a prospective assignment epoch (CR-3). It also tests whether withholding a receipt changes the decision and probes whether the specification itself is legible to its authors.

## Evidence and findings

The authors report 1,392 historical records (600 deterministic and 792 post-generation), detection of 11/11 injected semantic faults with 0/8 false positives in one check, and 30 prospective assignments that pass coverage/accounting checks. Withholding one receipt yields `INCONCLUSIVE_COVERAGE`; instrumentation adds about 0.021% inference time and 9.9 KB per transaction. A specification-legibility probe found ambiguity in the authors' own wording, an important limitation to the claimed guarantees.

## Reusable patterns

- Bind every aggregate claim to a machine-checkable evidence set and explicit coverage denominator.
- Use an inconclusive state for missing evidence rather than treating absence as a pass.
- Measure verifier overhead and specification legibility alongside fault-detection accuracy.

## Limitations and open questions

The records and fault model are transaction-centric, and the paper does not establish robustness to colluding agents, forged provenance, or delayed social outcomes. The small prospective epoch and self-reported legibility probe need independent implementations and tests on trajectory, population, and norm claims.

## Relation to this map

ClaimReceipt complements trajectory-judge and ledger-style records by adding evidence coverage to the claim boundary. A social simulator could use the same protocol to certify that a reported norm or population statistic is backed by all required episodes.
