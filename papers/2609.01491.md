## One-line takeaway

GlossoGen shows how pressured, partially informed LLM teams can develop productive but human-incomprehensible communication conventions, with public code for replay and intervention.

## Research object

- **World or environment:** SaveVeyru, a complex scenario in which agents must coordinate under pressure while holding partial information.
- **Agents:** Teams of LLM agents with different model strengths, plus newly introduced agents tested on an already-emerged convention.
- **Observations and actions:** Agents exchange messages through the scenario's communication channels, infer meanings from use, and may negotiate conventions during a postmortem stage.
- **Goals and interaction protocol:** Coordinate efficiently, measure language change, and test transmission and learning across population and training conditions.

## What the paper does

The authors provide the GlossoGen platform and vary efficiency pressure, model strength, and access to postmortem convention agreement. They analyse the structure of the resulting codes and whether agents can learn them from subsequent use.

## Evidence and findings

The arXiv abstract reports compositional and morphologically productive conventions that depart from the models' English prior and become difficult for humans to understand. It also reports that stronger models are needed for novel emergence, while weaker models can learn an existing language from usage alone. These are preprint findings; the linked repositories make the experimental substrate inspectable but do not constitute independent replication.

## Reusable patterns

- Log raw messages, channel visibility, and convention changes rather than only translated summaries.
- Separate emergence, transmission, and human-legibility tests.
- Use population swaps, postmortems, and controlled pressure changes as causal interventions.

## Limitations and open questions

Results come from one designed scenario and may depend on prompts, model families, message budgets, and the convention-analysis metric. Human monitoring failure, prompt injection through emergent codes, and stability under exogenous world changes remain open.

## Relation to this map

GlossoGen is a direct bridge between multi-agent social simulation and safety: it makes culture and information boundaries experimentally visible while showing why a fluent transcript is not necessarily a monitorable protocol.
