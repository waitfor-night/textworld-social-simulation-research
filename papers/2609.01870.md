## One-line takeaway

ArcticSwarm delays shared consensus until evidence has been gathered and reviewed, reducing early anchoring in long-horizon multi-agent research.

## Research object

- **World or environment:** BrowseComp-Plus and live BrowseComp web-research tasks.
- **Agents:** Parallel research agents with isolated search contexts, a shared bulletin board, and an integration/review stage.
- **Observations and actions:** Agents search and read evidence, publish findings to a controlled board, and cross commitment boundaries before a synthesizer forms the answer.
- **Goals and interaction protocol:** Improve research accuracy by separating evidence gathering, integration, and final commitment while preserving useful cross-agent evidence.

## What the paper does

The paper introduces staged information boundaries and three commitment points. It compares full isolation and review against variants that expose early findings or disable review, then tests the design with different model providers.

## Evidence and findings

The authors report BrowseComp-Plus accuracy of 82.6 for Qwen3.5-27B with the full design, versus 78.8 without isolation, 74.5 with review disabled, and 70.6 for MiroFlow. On live BrowseComp they report 73.6 for GPT-5 versus 54.9 for the provider baseline and 63.4 for MiroFlow. These are author-reported comparisons with different providers and no linked implementation, so they are evidence for the proposed ablations rather than a replication result.

## Reusable patterns

- Make early evidence visibility and consensus timing explicit experimental variables.
- Keep search contexts independent until a logged integration stage.
- Record which evidence crossed each commitment boundary so anchoring and correlated errors can be audited.

## Limitations and open questions

The evaluation is web-research-specific and does not test social worlds, private beliefs, or adversarial collusion. Provider/model comparisons may not be matched, and the missing public implementation limits replay. It remains open whether delayed consensus helps when agents share a changing world state or must negotiate norms.

## Relation to this map

ArcticSwarm supplies an information-boundary pattern that can be combined with authoritative social state, belief projections, and trajectory auditing. It is a direct design reference for preventing premature convergence in multi-agent text-world episodes.
