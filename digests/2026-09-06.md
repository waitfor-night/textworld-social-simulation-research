## Newly durable primary records

All dates below are the source's arXiv submission dates. Confidence is the repository's confidence in the triage and source interpretation, not a claim of independent replication. Every record was observed and deduplicated on 2026-09-06.

### State, beliefs, and information boundaries

- [Classic AI Scaffolding for LLM Social Agents](../papers/2609.01167.md) ([arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01167)) — submitted 2026-09-01; `SIM/core`; confidence `medium`. EpisodeSim makes roles, obligations, material state, timing, commitments, and closure persistent control state under a World Master. In a small author-adjudicated pilot, the full scaffold reports 0/6 major scene failures versus 6/6 for each minimal and grounded variant; the result is a design diagnostic, not a general benchmark.
- [Towards a Belief-Based World Model for LLM Agents](../papers/2609.00455.md) ([arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00455)) — submitted 2026-08-31; `TWM/core`; confidence `medium`. A queryable belief layer is separated from action simulation in partially observed ALFWorld and ScienceWorld tasks, with public reproduction code. The paper's performance and ablation numbers are author-reported and the hand-designed state model remains domain-specific.
- [ArcticSwarm: Deferring Early Consensus in Long-Horizon Multi-Agent Research](../papers/2609.01870.md) ([arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01870)) — submitted 2026-09-01; `MAS/core`; confidence `medium`. Isolated search contexts, a shared bulletin board, and staged commitment/review are tested on BrowseComp-Plus and live BrowseComp. The reported gains support delayed consensus as an ablation hypothesis, but provider comparisons and the absence of a linked implementation limit replay.

### Social interaction, culture, and population validity

- [GlossoGen: Emergent Language in Complex Multi-Agent LLM Interactions](../papers/2609.01491.md) ([arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01491)) — submitted 2026-09-01; `MAS/core`; confidence `medium`. The SaveVeyru platform exposes partial observations, communication channels, replay/fork/swap interventions, and transmission tests. The authors report productive conventions under pressure and postmortem access, including human-inscrutable codes; the evidence comes from a small fictional, cooperative setting without adversarial populations or human baselines.
- [Benchmarking large language model agent societies against human behavioural distributions (SILICA)](../papers/2608.28182.md) ([arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28182)) — submitted 2026-08-28; `SIM/core`; confidence `medium`. Five human-anchored social/economic environments, seeded open-weight runs, representation/design perturbations, and payoff-divergence controls separate first-round fit from end-state and interaction validity. The paper links code, transcripts, a reproducible release, and an OSF deposit, but anchors and exploratory cells still require independent audit.

### Memory and long-horizon control

- [CHIME: Credit-Aware Hierarchical Memory Evolution for Long-Horizon Agentic Planning](../papers/2609.02074.md) ([arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02074)) — submitted 2026-09-02; `AGT/core`; confidence `medium`. Planning and execution memories are separated and outcomes are attributed before writing. Across four listed long-horizon benchmarks, the authors report average gains, a reduction from 3,585 to 129 retained memories in one comparison, and attribution agreement up to 97.9%; the promised CHIME implementation was not independently located.
- [Learning What to Retain: Gated-Memory Routing for Efficient Collaboration in Multi-Agent LLM Systems](../papers/2609.00237.md) ([arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00237)) — submitted 2026-08-31; `MAS/adjacent`; confidence `medium`. Learned write, retrieval, role/backbone selection, and halting gates improve reported reasoning/code scores and cost. The linked repository was checked but exposed only a license at observation time, and the closed-domain evaluation does not establish safe retention of social facts or norms.
- [Invalidation Contracts for Cross-Episode Agent Memory](../papers/2609.00243.md) ([arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00243)) — submitted 2026-08-31; `AGT/adjacent`; confidence `medium`. Versioned, row-level invalidation separates memory validity from planner compliance across 9,432 API-recovery episodes. Companion server/client artifacts make the protocol inspectable, while licensing/provenance differ and the synthetic API scope leaves interpersonal drift untested.

### Evaluation and evidence integrity

- [trajectory-judge: What Outcome-Only LLM Judges Miss on Agent Trajectories](../papers/2609.00038.md) ([arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00038)) — submitted 2026-08-29; `EVAL/core`; confidence `medium`. A deterministic support-desk fault injector labels silent versus loud process failures and evaluates five judges over 400 trajectories. Outcome-only judging reports 45% silent-fault recall and 33% clean false alarms, while a step-aware judge improves silent recall at higher cost; the narrow scripted domain and single-fault design limit transfer.
- [ClaimReceipt: Verifying Evidence Sufficiency and Coverage in Agent Evaluations](../papers/2609.01992.md) ([arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01992)) — submitted 2026-09-02; `EVAL/adjacent`; confidence `medium`. Claim-relative receipts and an explicit `INCONCLUSIVE` state test whether an aggregate evaluation is backed by a complete evidence set. The authors report 1,392 historical records and 30 prospective assignments with injected-fault and coverage checks, but the transaction-centric protocol and specification-legibility issue need independent implementations.

## Recurring methods and environments

1. **Authority outside prose.** EpisodeSim's World Master, BB-WM's belief projection, ArcticSwarm's visibility boundaries, and Invalidation Contracts' version scopes all make a hidden control layer explicit. A social episode should expose authoritative state, per-agent beliefs, allowed evidence, and invalidation conditions separately from generated dialogue.
2. **Lifecycle-managed memory.** CHIME addresses credit before writing; Gated-Memory addresses write/retrieve/stop decisions; Invalidation Contracts address staleness. Together they imply a memory record of provenance, scope, utility or credit, retention decision, and invalidation rule—not an undifferentiated transcript.
3. **Intervention-ready interaction substrates.** GlossoGen's replay/fork/swap hooks, SILICA's representation and payoff perturbations, and trajectory-judge's fault injector turn apparently social behaviour into testable counterfactuals. Fixed seeds, raw event logs, and visibility traces should be default outputs.
4. **Bounded episodes and staged commitment.** EpisodeSim and ArcticSwarm both treat timing and commitment boundaries as causal variables. This is useful for the map's planned text-world harness, where premature consensus, delayed obligations, and closure failures can be measured directly.

## Evaluation patterns becoming common

- **Process versus outcome:** trajectory-judge separates silent outcome-surviving faults from loud failures; ClaimReceipt separates valid, invalid, and insufficiently covered claims. Social evaluations should report both what happened and whether the route and evidence were valid.
- **Perturbation and contamination controls:** SILICA changes presentation, ordering, labels, and incentives to test whether a result survives apparatus changes or is recalled from a prior. GlossoGen varies pressure, postmortem access, and agent swaps. These are stronger than a single prompt-and-score run.
- **Population and distributional checks:** SILICA compares trajectories and end states to human distributions rather than judging one plausible dialogue. GlossoGen measures emergence, transmission, and legibility separately. Individual role consistency, group coordination, and population validity should remain distinct metrics.
- **Cost and coverage accounting:** CHIME and Gated-Memory report memory or inference cost; ClaimReceipt reports evidence coverage and verifier overhead; trajectory-judge reports wall-clock judging cost. A headline score without denominator, instrumentation, or cost is not enough for this map.

## Disagreements, weak claims, and missing baselines

- **Coherence is not realism.** EpisodeSim's tiny qualitative pilot and GlossoGen's successful conventions show that explicit scaffolds can stabilize an interaction, but neither establishes human-like social dynamics. SILICA's weak end-state agreement is a useful counterweight to fluent demonstrations.
- **Memory gains may trade against omission.** Credit-aware and gated retention can reduce noise and cost, while dropping a low-frequency relationship, safety, or norm fact could be more harmful than retaining redundant context. None of the promoted papers tests this fully in a changing social world.
- **Artifacts are uneven.** BB-WM, GlossoGen, SILICA, trajectory-judge, and the invalidation client provide inspectable material; CHIME's promised release and Gated-Memory's linked but currently sparse repository are not yet equivalent to a reproducible package. Claims should be weighted accordingly.
- **Comparability is fragile.** Several papers compare different providers, backbones, prompts, or benchmark slices. Author-reported improvements are not treated as independent replications, and no promoted item yet supplies a cross-cultural human anchor for open-ended social episodes.
