# Web Signals Digest: Text Worlds, Social Simulation, and Agent Evaluation

- **Date:** 2026-09-04
- **Scan window:** 2026-08-28 through 2026-09-04
- **Observation date:** 2026-09-04 (Asia/Shanghai)
- **Method:** Primary-source scan of arXiv records and official project/workshop pages, supplemented by news, repository, and public social-media discovery. Abstract-level claims are attributed to the authors; no private sources or personal contact details were used.

## Executive summary

This run adds 14 dated signal notes: 12 new primary-paper records and two clearly marked early signals. No arXiv identifier or canonical URL duplicated the 11 records already in papers/index.csv or the existing role-playing product note. The paper index was left unchanged because this run is web-signal triage; durable paper records can be promoted after code and full-text review.

The strongest shift since the prior literature/product snapshot is from “agents that can produce plausible dialogue” toward explicit control and measurement layers:

- world authority, episode closure, and uncertainty are being represented as first-class state;
- social validity is being tested against human distributions, rule-preserving perturbations, and process-level faults;
- memory is becoming a lifecycle with credit assignment, invalidation, retention, and retrieval policies;
- emergent language, market behaviour, hybrid creativity, and emotional strategies show why population-level and persona-level metrics must remain separate.

The results are promising but early. Most empirical items are single preprints with author-reported numbers, and several code releases or benchmark details still need to be checked.

## Confirmed developments from primary sources

“Confirmed” here means that a dated paper or public artifact exists and its stated claim can be checked at the source. It does not mean that the reported result has independent replication.

### World models and text environments

- [EpisodeSim: classic-AI scaffolding for social episodes](../signals/2026-09-04-episode-sim-classic-ai-scaffolding.md) (2026-09-01) makes roles, obligations, material state, timing, and closure persistent control state under a World Master. The small qualitative ablation is a design signal to reproduce, not a finished benchmark.
- [Belief-Based World Models](../signals/2026-09-04-belief-based-world-model.md) (2026-08-31) expose known-versus-uncertain state to an LLM policy in addition to simulation, with public ALFWorld and ScienceWorld code.
- [Environment Evolution for Terminal Agents](../signals/2026-09-04-environment-evolution-terminal-agents.md) (2026-09-03) uses off-policy, multi-agent environment generation to keep terminal tasks difficult and reports gains on Terminal-Bench 2.1.

### Social and multi-agent phenomena

- [SILICA](../signals/2026-09-04-silica-agent-societies-benchmark.md) (2026-08-28) proposes human-anchored, perturbation-tested agent-society certification and reports weak end-state agreement despite some first-round matches.
- [GlossoGen](../signals/2026-09-04-glossogen-emergent-language.md) (2026-09-01) reports emergent, sometimes human-incomprehensible conventions in pressured partial-information teams and provides a platform with event logs and interventions.
- [Competitive Market Behavior of LLMs](../signals/2026-09-04-competitive-market-llm-agents.md) (2026-09-02) places agents in a double auction and reports slower convergence and lower efficiency than human markets.
- [Collective creativity in hybrid societies](../signals/2026-09-04-collective-creativity-hybrid-societies.md) (2026-09-02) separates individual novelty from population diversity and treats composition and connectivity as causal design variables for mixed human-machine groups.

### Memory, planning, and long-horizon control

- [CHIME](../signals/2026-09-04-chime-memory-evolution.md) (2026-09-02) separates planning and execution memories and attributes outcomes before writing, addressing a credit-assignment failure in self-evolving memory.
- [Invalidation Contracts](../signals/2026-09-04-invalidation-contracts-memory.md) (2026-08-31) attaches version and cacheability metadata to cross-episode memories so stale entries can be evicted precisely.
- [Gated-Memory Routing](../signals/2026-09-04-gated-memory-routing.md) (2026-08-31) learns what to write and retrieve in multi-agent execution histories and reports accuracy/cost improvements on five reasoning and code benchmarks.

### Persona and evaluation safety

- [Emotional Labor Strategy Preferences in LLM Personas](../signals/2026-09-04-emotional-labor-llm-personas.md) (2026-08-31) introduces a 500-event, three-strategy dataset and reports trait- and model-dependent persona effects.
- [trajectory-judge](../signals/2026-09-04-trajectory-judge.md) (2026-08-29) releases a deterministic fault-injection environment showing why outcome-only judges miss silent process failures and unsupported final claims.

## What changed since the prior repository snapshot

The previous dated snapshot combined earlier literature with a product landscape. This run adds current evidence in five places:

1. **Authority and uncertainty:** EpisodeSim, BB-WM, and the open prototype make the environment's state, projection, or belief boundary explicit rather than leaving it to generated prose.
2. **Validity beyond fluency:** SILICA adds human-distribution and apparatus-perturbation checks; trajectory-judge adds silent-versus-loud process faults; Environment Evolution treats task difficulty as an evolving experimental variable.
3. **Memory as a managed system:** CHIME, Invalidation Contracts, and Gated-Memory Routing cover attribution, staleness, selection, and cost. This extends the earlier product observation that memory is retrieval/compression, not an infinite transcript.
4. **Population and culture effects:** GlossoGen, market experiments, and hybrid-creativity work show distinct collective outcomes—conventions, efficiency, and diversity—that should not be collapsed into a single “social realism” score.
5. **Persona-specific behaviour:** Emotional-labor choices give the map a measurable social-strategy task alongside role consistency and opinion dynamics.

## Cross-cutting patterns to carry into the research map

| Pattern | New evidence | Implication for this project |
|---|---|---|
| Authoritative state versus rendered text | EpisodeSim, BB-WM, social-agent-sim | Keep world state, beliefs, action validation, and narrative rendering as inspectable layers. |
| Partial observability and visibility | BB-WM, GlossoGen, SILICA | Log what each agent could know, not only what it said; test leakage and order sensitivity. |
| Individual success versus collective validity | SILICA, market, hybrid-creativity papers | Report trajectory, group, and population metrics separately, with human anchors where possible. |
| Process and final-message auditing | trajectory-judge, GlossoGen event logs | Preserve raw events and inspect both how an outcome was reached and what was finally asserted. |
| Memory lifecycle | CHIME, Invalidation Contracts, Gated-Memory Routing | Give every memory a provenance, utility/credit signal, retention rule, and invalidation path. |
| Difficulty and intervention control | Environment Evolution, GlossoGen fork/swap hooks | Use fixed seeds and controlled perturbations so social claims survive replay and counterfactual tests. |

## Limits and confidence

The source existence and dates are high-confidence for the linked arXiv and official pages. The empirical conclusions are medium-confidence triage signals because they are new, author-reported preprints; several lack public code, independent replications, or enough methodological detail in the abstract. The two repository/event notes are intentionally marked signal, and the prototype note is low confidence because its release date and run provenance were not visible.

## Recommended follow-ups

1. Reproduce the smallest public runs for BB-WM, GlossoGen, and trajectory-judge; pin model, prompt, seed, and benchmark versions.
2. Build a compact social-world testbed combining EpisodeSim-style authoritative episodes, private beliefs, explicit memory provenance, and deterministic event logs.
3. Add SILICA-style human-distribution, order-perturbation, and end-state checks, plus trajectory-judge-style injected failures and final-message audits.
4. Test CHIME, invalidation, and gated retrieval on relationship, norm, and belief memories; measure useful retention and harmful omission separately.
5. Monitor the LASS deadline and post-acceptance program for new public benchmarks, datasets, and safety protocols.
