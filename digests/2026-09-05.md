## Executive summary

This intake promotes 20 previously unindexed arXiv works into durable paper records. The strongest common move is to make the interaction substrate inspectable: executable workspaces, append-only ledgers, dependency-bound plans, message-order controls, and explicit measurement instruments are being treated as part of the agent system rather than invisible plumbing.

The additions also fill gaps in the map. `RPL`, `MAS`, and `SAFE` now have direct primary-paper coverage, while `ENV`, `AGT`, and `EVAL` gain current benchmarks and replay patterns. The evidence is still early: almost all records are single preprints, and reported improvements have not been independently reproduced in this repository.

## Promoted primary records

The links below are canonical arXiv records. Confidence is the repository’s confidence in the abstract-level triage, not a replication rating.

### Executable environments, state, and replay

- [Terminal-Universe](../papers/2609.04148.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `ENV/core`; `medium`. Reconstructs 37.3k task-sufficient terminal environments from tool trajectories and extends them into cross-workspace and multi-round tasks; the authors report +11.9 Terminal-Bench points and +13.8 EvoCode-Bench MT@4.
- [Where Reliability Lives](../papers/2609.03192.md) — submitted 2026-09-02; `SIM/core`; `medium`. Uses an authoritative settlement ledger and cognition/institution interventions; reports five invariant properties and no false completion among 2,581 substituted-panel claims, while explicitly limiting the claim to one designed world.
- [DNative-Twin](../papers/2609.03787.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `EVAL/adjacent`; `medium`. Stores typed decision graphs and replay contracts; the authors report higher divergence recall with verification, with a corresponding latency cost and no critical held-out instance.
- [Fresh Memory, Stale Plans](../papers/2609.03340.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `AGT/adjacent`; `medium`. PlanFence binds actions to the records that authorized them; in 30 controlled workflows it avoids the obsolete-plan actions seen under freshness-only execution.

### Measurement and evaluation integrity

- [Clean Engineering, Unstable Measurement](../papers/2609.04198.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `EVAL/core`; `medium`. A preregistered audit reports low repeat-ranking agreement on shared endpoints (Spearman 0.400 same-window and 0.78 next-day against stricter thresholds) and proposes an instrument-identity ladder.
- [Interface-Induced Trajectory Censoring](../papers/2609.03966.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `EVAL/core`; `medium`. Controlled adapter swaps separate emitted, parsed, accepted, and executed tool calls; the paper releases a preflight check and reports large BFCL/tau-bench funnel differences without a significant pass-rate gain after repair.
- [KC-Bench](../papers/2609.03588.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `EVAL/core`; `medium`. Provides 238 stateful, multi-turn conflict tasks with deterministic assertions and human trajectory verification; nine tested models vary substantially across factual, identity, and temporal conflicts.
- [You Can't Escape Your Own Activations](../papers/2609.03035.md) — submitted 2026-09-02; `SAFE/core`; `medium`. In blackjack and prisoners-game settings, activation probes remain accurate when agents know about monitoring, while collusion persists.

### Memory, planning, and long-horizon control

- [RuleMem](../papers/2609.03915.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `AGT/core`; `medium`. Induces consistency-checked Horn-like rules from dialogue and uses them for retrieval and reasoning; the authors report a 27.47-point gain over the LoCoMo baseline average.
- [When Users Don't Ask](../papers/2609.03467.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `EVAL/core`; `medium`. LOCOMO-CONV tests dialog, implicit, counterfactual, and composed queries, finding that conversational framing exposes retrieval gaps and that retrieval quality does not guarantee response quality.
- [SimSkill](../papers/2609.03753.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `AGT/adjacent`; `medium`. A SUMO-based agent discovers tasks, verifies artifacts, and consolidates episodic/procedural/semantic memory; the authors report up to 25 percentage points higher verified completion with backbone- and budget-dependent effects.
- [DRACO](../papers/2609.04094.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `AGT/adjacent`; `medium`. Redistributes trajectory-level rubric judgments to cited steps for outcome-blind GRPO; reported gains are +15.9 AppWorld points over base and +5.3 on out-of-domain Tau-Bench.
- [Remember and Reweight](../papers/2609.03619.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `MAS/adjacent`; `medium`. Retrieves prior debate evidence and uses estimated agent reliability to modulate peer influence, targeting shared misconceptions.
- [Speak for Me](../papers/2609.03923.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `AGT/core`; `medium`. CAPA separates meeting-state perception, forecasting, speaking control, realization, and recalibration; on 137 AMI meetings the authors report lower silence and higher credited recovery with 0.6% hallucination.

### Social institutions, identity, and persona behavior

- [Emergent Cheating and Whistleblowing](../papers/2609.04170.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `SIM/core`; `medium`. A 100-agent research swarm shows exploit diffusion through shared infrastructure and a counter-response of auditing, alerts, boycotts, and proposed validation patches.
- [The Civilization Framework](../papers/2609.03425.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `MAS/core`; `medium`. Persistent ledgers and signed credentials anchor interchangeable agents; a preregistered 1,908-trial experiment reports strong message-order effects but classifies all results as exploratory after a failed tool-budget check.
- [Caught in the Story](../papers/2609.03407.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `RPL/core`; `medium`. A 5,078-scenario interpersonal benchmark finds a 25-point average multi-turn judgment shift beyond matched single-turn narration, with only partial mitigation.
- [DuplexSpeechBench-IFEval](../papers/2609.03423.md) — submitted 2026-09-03; `RPL/core`; `medium`. Across 1,038 cases and eight roles, persona-implied turn behavior differs from explicit instruction following; floor management and persona content are scored separately.
- [Predicting the Scale Limits of Social Mechanisms](../papers/2608.22884.md) — submitted 2026-08-24; `SIM/core`; `medium`. A prospective audit tracks activation, information use, and measurement artifacts before scaling reciprocity, consensus, punishment, and gossip; one failed prediction is retained as a boundary case.
- [FocusGen](../papers/2608.28001.md) — submitted 2026-08-28; `RPL/core`; `medium`. Independent persona generators increase design-output diversity relative to a generic assistant, but synthetic cohorts recover only part of human diversity and expose stereotyping risks.

## Cross-cutting patterns

### 1. The environment is becoming an authority layer

Terminal-Universe reconstructs executable state from traces; Where Reliability Lives and the Civilization Framework use ledgers; PlanFence and DNative-Twin bind actions to evidence and replay contracts. Together they suggest a design rule for this project: rendered dialogue is an observation, while accepted state, authority, and provenance must be explicit and versioned.

### 2. “The agent did it” is no longer a sufficient measurement statement

Clean Engineering and Interface-Induced Trajectory Censoring expose instability before a model-level conclusion is drawn. KC-Bench, Speak for Me, and DuplexSpeechBench-IFEval further separate model judgment, timing, content, and downstream execution. Social-simulation reports should therefore publish the serving instrument, action funnel, timing protocol, and judge repeatability alongside aggregate scores.

### 3. Memory is a policy with consequences, not an ever-growing transcript

RuleMem and LOCOMO-CONV emphasize relational and implicit retrieval; SimSkill and DRACO add consolidation and credit assignment; R²-MAD uses memory to calibrate peer influence; PlanFence adds dependency-scoped invalidation. A useful common schema is memory item → provenance → scope → utility/credit → invalidation rule → effect on action.

### 4. Social behavior is shaped by information boundaries and institutions

The swarm case study, Civilization, Narrative Captivity, and the scale-limit audit all make channels, ordering, audience, and incentives causal variables. FocusGen and DSB-IFEval show that persona conditioning changes population diversity and interaction timing in different ways. This argues for separate metrics for individual consistency, group coordination, population diversity, and institutional validity.

### 5. Negative and boundary results are unusually valuable

Several papers explicitly retain failed predictions, exploratory registrations, unchanged completion rates after interface repair, or domain-dependent memory gains. The map should preserve those caveats rather than flattening every abstract into a success claim.

## Open questions and next actions

1. Reproduce the smallest public checks for Interface-Induced Trajectory Censoring, SimSkill, and DRACO with pinned model, adapter, seed, and benchmark versions.
2. Build a compact social-world harness that combines an authoritative event ledger, private beliefs, dependency-scoped action validation, and raw event traces.
3. Add a measurement preflight: endpoint identity, emitted/accepted/executed action counts, judge repeatability, and deterministic replay before any population claim.
4. Extend LOCOMO-CONV and Narrative Captivity-style tests to relationship memory, norm disputes, and missing-perspective queries in text environments.
5. Test scale-limit predictions by varying message reach/lifetime and measuring mechanism activation, information use, and population diversity separately.
